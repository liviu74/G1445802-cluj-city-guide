About Cluj-Napoca


Cluj-Napoca is the unofficial capital of the Transylvania region in Romania. Known as a major university, cultural, and tech hub, the city blends historic Austro-Hungarian architecture with a lively, youthful atmosphere.

Useful Visitor Information (Click to expand)

Originally a Roman settlement called Napoca, the city grew into a major medieval trading center and today preserves landmarks like St. Michael's Church and the Tailors' Bastion.

Cluj-Napoca hosts famous international events such as Untold Festival, Electric Castle, and the Transilvania International Film Festival (TIFF).

The city center is compact and easy to explore on foot. Public buses and trams connect all major neighborhoods, and Cluj International Airport is only 20 minutes from the center.
